When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Eagan

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
